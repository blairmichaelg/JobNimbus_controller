## 2. Your Core Responsibilities & Workflow

Once you log in, you will be taken directly to the **Accounting Ledger**. Here is what you'll do on a daily basis:

### Tracking ACV and Supplement Checks
Unlike the old system where we just checked a box, you will now enter the **actual dollar amounts** for checks.
1. Find a job under the ACV or Supplement section that is currently marked **"Pending."**
2. Click it and enter the exact dollar amount from the physical check you received, along with the date.
3. The system acts as a safeguard: it compares the check amount against the expected RCV or Depreciation. If the check is short by 2% or more, the system will flag a warning to you so you can follow up with the insurance carrier!

### Paying Canvasser Commissions
The brand new Commission Engine is now live and fully automated! 
- Every canvasser earns a default **10% of total revenue** on a job. 
- You can override this on a per-job basis by clicking **"Adjust %"** (e.g., if you have a special arrangement of 12% for a specific job).
- Need a paper trail? Click **"Download PDF"** next to a rep's name to generate an automated, professional commission statement document for payout records.

### QuickBooks Online (QBO) Exports
- Look at the **"QBO Export Queue"** metric card at the top of your dashboard to see how many jobs are waiting to be exported.
- Click **"Export QBO CSV"** to download a ready-to-import file with all the new jobs.
- **Safeguard:** Once you export a job, the system marks it exported. You can never accidentally double-export a job to QuickBooks!

## 3. Transparency & Visibility
While you don't have access to the Admin controls or Operations scheduling, you **DO** have full access to view any job's complete profile. 
- Click **"View Details →"** on any job row to see the full financial breakdown, margins, measurements, and all documents (like the Contingency Agreement or generated Invoices).
